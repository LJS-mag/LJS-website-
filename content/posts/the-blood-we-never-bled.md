You wake up after a long night of scrolling, absorbing brain rot. Or maybe you wake up from a tempest of unbound thoughts that refused you sleep. Either way, the routine demands obedience. So, you obey. 
You perform the motions of a morning.
You bid your parents goodbye.
You answer the mocking faces of your siblings with tired smirk.
You step out of your house.
Now look closely. 
See.
There are thousands like you.
Their faces differ. Their worries differ too.
But the triviality of their worries remains the same.
Some are consumed by Future. Others shackled to the past.
Some are scrambling to earn money. Others are drowned in the lust of money.
Some are perturbed over exams. Others are exhausted from grading exams.
Some are crushed beneath their parents’ high expectations. Others are parents who have ceased to expect anything at all.
Then you will see some whose prayers are only for their interests.  Others young in body, but waiting to pray until their bones are too old to bow.
Do you see the dissonance? 
Follow me. I’ll show you absurdity and selfishness.
There is another world, but little out of our curated, colourful Bubble.
Let’s zoom out a bit.
Look closely…it’s exactly near our world.
1.2.3.4.
Children. Women.
Raped.
Silenced. Buried in case files no one will open.
1.2.3.4. to a count we refused to measure.
Women.
Shot in the name of honour.
No justice served.
This is our country-Pakistan.
1.2.3.4
Children shot dead.
Women violated.
Men executed.
Families crumpled beneath tanks.
Homes bombed.
And we just scroll past.
This is our Palestine.
1.2.3.4.
Shot. Dead. Starvation.
The blood spilled in such a volume that even satellite maps bleed red.
This is our Sudan.
Then there is Afghanistan, Lebanon, and the list goes on.
This is the blood we never bled-so we cared very little.
If I ask you about the latest Instagram trend, you’ll recite them all without hesitation.
But we don’t know what is happening out of our sphere-just a little bit away from our vibrant world.
Blood and truth don’t match our vibe.
They don’t fit our aesthetic.
World’s major concerns are categorized as ours and not ours.
This is absurdity.
Most of us bow before our creator Allah. We prostrate. We pray.
But our prayers are transactions, petitions for this world, for Jannah, for ourselves and for those bound to us by blood.
But never for the Muslims in Palestine. Never for Sudan. Never for Lebanon. And this is selfishness.
Do you remember the Hadith?
"Whoever among you sees evil, let him change it with his hand. If he cannot do so, then with his tongue. If he cannot do so, then with his heart, which is the weakest level of faith."
Our Iman is being tested in this era. We cannot change the evil with our hands but we aren’t even retraining our desires either to boycott. We aren’t even changing it with our tongues-even in an age where a single video can reach millions.
Because it isn’t aesthetic.
Because it doesn’t trend.
So, we settle for the weakest level of faith.
Allah is Al Adl. The Just.
And when we will stand before Him, perhaps we will be able to say, “we prayed.” But if He asked us about our Muslim Brothers-about what we did to help them. I am sure none of us will be able to lift our heads.
Do not normalise injustice.
Do not normalise genocide.
