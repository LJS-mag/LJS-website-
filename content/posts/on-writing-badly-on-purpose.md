Finishing school doesn't feel like winning a race with accolades and cheers waiting for you at the end. It feels like crossing an invisible line from the ground up into the air. You're suspended on a tightrope submerged in a moody lake. One wrong move and you sink deep into the endless abyss, escaping the rays of summer.

Summer is painted with the picturesque essence of friends that is so incredibly comforting. They surround you like petals blowing in the breeze reminding you of freedom and connection. Except you don’t want those petals to aimlessly float around you. You want them to choke you. To suffocate you until all you know is their nauseating scent and sickening taste and not even a phantom of your own thoughts.

As soon as it happens though, you attempt to claw those petals off your throat, scratching nothing but your soul.

How do you explain that to someone?

That with the endless ‘freedom’ of this very special summer you want to be suffocated and unchained all at once?

You lay awake at night, freedom cradled softly in your hands. Freedom that can be taken away if you so much as blink so you exploit it. You exploit your freedom before anyone else can.

The tightrope shakes and the petals swirl, reminding you of your privilege to hold talent and your mortality. *You are everything* and yet you are nothing.

Hearts are stitched with the thread of softness but then you grow up. Poke. Poke. Poke. *Stab*. Your heart has holes. You are good and young and healthy and free until you look around. There's that person you hurt beyond repair. There's that lady you did not say thank you to. There's that failed exam, that unopened college application. And look! Your freedom has been snatched when your head was turnt.

Then you look the other way. A girl at a poolside who grows to love her skin because of your insistent words. You never see her again but your words hug her during her darkest hours. There are people who have been inspired by the fabric of your soul in ways you will never know. People who did not feel seen before they met you.

You turn your head back and find your freedom staring you dead in the face without a single blink. It cannot be snatched because it has always belonged to you and solely you. They can tug at it all they want but they can never truly take it.

The college application sits open on your laptop. On the left side there is that failed exam. But on the right side there's a pile of your achievements breaking through the ceiling.

The room is filled with people you have helped and hurt. The summer may seem fenced in by your doubts and fears but the fence doesn't exist. The fence is a wispy hallucination.

All you have to do is put one step in front of the other on the tightrope with the petals guiding your way and before you know it you’ve passed through that deeply frightening fence.

Because it was never there.
